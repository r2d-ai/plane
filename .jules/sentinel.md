## 2025-05-18 - Open Redirect & Injection Bypass in `isValidNextPath`
**Vulnerability:** `isValidNextPath` in `@plane/utils` checked `startsWith("//")` and `includes("\\")` on raw input strings without decoding percent-encoded characters or checking query/hash parameters. Attacker-controlled paths like `/%2f%2fevil.com`, `/%5cevil.com`, or query parameters containing `<script>` or event handlers were permitted.
**Learning:** Checking URL strings for open redirect signatures before `decodeURIComponent` allows percent-encoding bypasses. Additionally, WHATWG URL parsing can strip or normalize control characters (such as `\t`, `\r`, `\n`) or change hosts if backslashes or encoded slashes are decoded later by browsers.
**Prevention:** Always decode URI components (`decodeURIComponent`), check for control/whitespace characters (`[\x00-\x1F\x7F]`), and validate that the URL constructor normalizes host, port, protocol, pathname, search, and hash without host-switching or malicious injection payloads.
